SETTINGS

If your character winks the wrong eye, turn on Swap face sides. If tracking feels slow on your phone, set Track to Face only or Face + arms. Recenter (R) sets your normal head position as "looking straight ahead."

MIRROR

Pick a character and it becomes your reflection. It blinks when you blink, smiles when you smile, turns its head with you, and waves when you wave. Nothing leaves your device.

Face the camera in good light, with your head and shoulders in view. No camera? You can still play: move the pointer to make your character look around, and hold to make it talk.
